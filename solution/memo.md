**Re:** First-response SLA breaches: who, which shift, and what to do

**Short answer.** No agent is the problem, and the morning team is not the problem. Since 30 June 2025 nobody has been rostered on the Night shift (22:00 to 06:00 IST), and that is where the breaches come from. It can be fixed by moving two people you already have, so it does not need a hire.

**What I found**
- Before 30 June 2025, 9.2% of tickets missed their first-response target. Since then it is 25.0%.
- Indore had five night agents. On 30 June two of them (Tarun Mishra, Harpreet Deshpande) moved to Day. The other three (Zoya Mehta, Deepak Jadhav, Shreya Bhatt) have no roster row after 29 June, so they either left or the roster is incomplete. Either way there is no Night row after 29 June, and the move was not headcount-neutral for that shift.
- Tickets created overnight now wait until the morning shift opens. **79% of them breach** (1,581 of 1,999). Tickets created while someone is on shift breach 9.4%, the same as before (9.2%).
- The standard report blames whoever *resolved* the ticket. Morning agents resolve the overnight backlog, so they carry 85% of all breaches. That is why the morning chat team see a wall of red. Judged only on tickets that arrived during their own shift, they breach about 6 to 9%, the same as everyone else.
- On that fair measure, **no agent is significantly worse than their peers.** One person looked high over the last 13 weeks (7 of 33 tickets). Over the full period they are in line, so the report marks it as "watch" and not as a finding.

**Why the credit line moved (Arjun, Priya)**
Every breach costs a Rs 350 store credit. Breaches went from about 34 a month in June 2025 to about 195 a month, and have stayed at that level since August. So Priya is right that it has been flat month to month since the summer, and Arjun is right that it stepped up sharply. It was a one-off step in July 2025, and the credits have run at the higher level ever since. Breached tickets also score lower on CSAT (2.8 against 3.5 for tickets that met target).

**Recommended action and number**
Put two existing agents on rotating Night cover (one chat and social, one email), taken from the Day shift, where the breach rate is 9% and would stay there. Target: **bring the overall breach rate from 25% back to about 9.5%.** On this export that avoids roughly 1,350 credits over the last 12 months, **about Rs 1.2 lakh a quarter**. The pack says the desk handles about 650 tickets a week, while the export averages about 170. If the export is a sample, the same fix is worth **about Rs 4.5 lakh a quarter**. I cannot tell from the pack which figure is right, so please confirm which is real. Hiring would not be worth it at the export volume (Rs 9,240 a week for one night agent against about Rs 9,000 a week of credits avoided). Moving people costs nothing.
